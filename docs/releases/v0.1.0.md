## Objective

Phase 1 of the [release plan](../roadmap/release-plan.md#phase-1--opendrive-vertical-slice):
open ASAM OpenDRIVE road networks (`.xodr`) through `Usd.Stage.Open` as a
read-only layer. Roads, lanes, and junctions are authored as semantics under
`/Asset/Network`, with centerlines and lane borders as derived geometry under
`/Asset/Visualization`. Input that cannot be represented fails with a stable
code. The [0.1.0 changelog](../../CHANGELOG.md#010---2026-11-23) lists what
ships.

## Shipped capabilities

The [capability matrix](../reference/CAPABILITY_MATRIX.md) at the tag is the
complete list. In short:

- OpenDRIVE `revMajor` 1 with `revMinor` 4 to 9.
- Line and arc plan views, elevation, lane offset, lane sections, widths,
  and types.
- Road and lane links, and `default` junctions.
- The georeference and offset, authored verbatim.
- Metadata-only reads, composition, and reload.
- Stable `ODR-` and `RN-` diagnostics with a loss summary on `/Asset`.
- An import limit of 512 MiB.

## Compatibility

- One package per target, for the OpenStrata `cy2026` / `usd` runtimes:
  Windows x86-64 (MSVC 143), Linux x86-64, and macOS arm64, with OpenUSD
  26.08 and Python 3.13
  ([supported configurations](../reference/SUPPORTED_CONFIGURATIONS.md)).
- The bundle manifest declares OpenUSD `>=26.08,<27.0`, but only 26.08 is
  exercised. Each package requires the runtime its manifest records.
- The plugin is read-only, takes no file-format arguments, and has no public
  C++ interface.

## Known limitations

- Spiral, `poly3`, and `paramPoly3` plan views, non-zero superelevation and
  lateral shape, lane borders, temporary lane layers, and `direct`,
  `virtual`, and `crossing` junctions are rejected with a code. Signals,
  objects, and road marks are not authored, and warnings count them.
- Storm draws networks far from the origin in steps of the `float` spacing
  (about 0.5 m at 5,400 km). The authored data keeps its precision
  ([REL-01 report §4](../reports/rel-01-inspection-2026-11-23.md#4-far-from-the-origin)).
- The SBOM lists the bundle and its files. It does not list pugixml, which
  is compiled in, because `ost` 0.23.21 has no field for it. Its license
  ships in `THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md`.
- Digests are shown to reproduce within one build job at fixed paths. The
  Linux and Windows digests also matched between the two dry runs, whose
  commits changed no code for those platforms. Reproducibility across
  machines or paths is not claimed.
- `usdview` (L6) was checked only on Windows.
