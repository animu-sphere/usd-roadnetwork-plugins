# ADR 0010 probes — 2026-11-24

This report records the measurements gathered for the ADR-10 work item in
[current work](../roadmap/current.md#active-work): the evaluation method and
error bound for `<spiral>` and `<paramPoly3>`, the meaning of an absent
`pRange`, the tessellation bounds for the new shapes and for superelevation,
and the golden values that
[ADR 0010](../adr/0010-plan-view-shapes-and-superelevation.md) fixes. It
builds on the [FMT-02 spike](format-spike-opendrive-2026-11-23.md), which
measured VIRES `odrSpiral` and the `paramPoly3` length mismatches; this
report does not repeat those. Nothing here is a support claim: the
[capability matrix](../reference/CAPABILITY_MATRIX.md) is unchanged.

## 1. Environment

- Local Windows 11, CPython 3.13.14 with NumPy 2.5.1 (for the
  Gauss–Legendre nodes) and mpmath 1.4.1 (for the references), installed
  outside the repository.
- The prototype evaluates in IEEE `double` with the Python `math` module, in
  the operation order the ADR states. It is not the C++ implementation: a
  compiler may contract or reorder operations, so GEO-02 repeats the checks
  in `roadnetwork-core`.
- The references use mpmath at 40 significant digits, with every input the
  `double` nearest its decimal text, as the decoder reads it. Spiral
  references integrate the phase with mpmath `quad` on pieces of at most
  0.5 rad.
- The probe scripts were throwaway scripts and are not committed. The
  methods are described in each section, so the results can be reproduced.

## 2. Spiral by piecewise quadrature

A spiral that starts at the origin with heading 0, curvature `κ0`, and
curvature rate `κ̇ = (curvEnd − curvStart) / length` has the phase
`φ(v) = κ0·v + κ̇·v²/2`, and its offset at distance `u` is the integral of
`(cos φ, sin φ)` from 0 to `u`. The prototype splits `[0, u]` into `m`
pieces of equal length `h`, with

```text
m = max(1, ceil(|u| · (K + sqrt|κ̇|) / B))      K = max(|κ0|, |κ0 + κ̇u|)
```

and integrates each piece with `n`-point Gauss–Legendre about its midpoint
`c`: the phase is `φ(c)` plus `w·(κ(c) + κ̇w/2)` for `w` in
`[−h/2, h/2]`, and the piece's sum is rotated by `φ(c)`. The cases:

- FMT-02 §4.1: `κ0 = 0`, `κ̇` from 1e-8 to 1 by factors of 10, both signs,
  at five distances up to a heading change of 2π;
- FMT-02 §4.2–§4.3: `κ0` in {0, 0.01, 0.1, 0.5, −0.2}, lengths 5, 50, 200,
  and 500 m, and `curvEnd − curvStart` in {0, 1e-16, 1e-14, 1e-11, 1e-10,
  1e-9, 1e-7, 1e-5, 1e-3, 1e-2, −1e-9, −1e-3, −1e-2}, at 0.3 and 1.0 of the
  length;
- three spirals whose curvature changes sign, and lengths of 1e-6 m, 10 km,
  and 30 km.

| `n` | `B` (rad) | Largest error | Largest error / `|u|` |
| ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 4 | 0.5 | 5.3e-8 m | 1.5e-11 |
| 4 | 1 | 8.0e-7 m | 5.6e-10 |
| 5 | 1 | 8.8e-10 m | 5.3e-13 |
| 6 | 1 | 6.1e-12 m | 9.2e-16 |
| 6 | 2 | 5.0e-10 m | 3.2e-12 |
| **8** | **1** | **6.2e-12 m** | **9.2e-16** |
| 8 | 2 | 8.5e-12 m | 1.0e-15 |
| 10 | 2 | 8.7e-12 m | 1.0e-15 |

The largest error with `n = 8`, `B = 1` is at `κ0 = 0.01`, `κ̇ = 1e-9`,
`u = 10` km, and is rounding: it does not fall with more points. The
truncation error falls by about three orders of magnitude for each point
added between 4 and 6, so with 8 points it is several orders of magnitude
below the rounding. The spirals on which the composition of FMT-02 §4.2
divides by zero (`curvStart == curvEnd`) or is wrong by 11.9 m (a
difference of 1e-16) are in the grid, with no larger error than the others.

The method needs no special case for a constant curvature: with `κ̇ = 0` it
integrates an arc, within the same error. The number of pieces grows with
the heading change of the segment, as the number of tessellation samples
does.

## 3. Absent `pRange`

FMT-02 §4.4 found 113 `paramPoly3` records without `pRange`, from SUMO
`netconvert` and unattributed 1.4 files, and the 1.9.0 specification states
no default (§6). The SMARTS `scenarios/open_drive/od_4lane/map.xodr` at
`c113dd7425e2` (MIT, SUMO `netconvert` 1.4; SHA-256
`79205642aa135ae53841b417b690f374c48bd1f0cbeb90edb1e49e37beebee58`, as in
[FMT-02 §10](format-spike-opendrive-2026-11-23.md#10-fixtures)) has eight
junction roads that are one `paramPoly3` each, without `pRange`, such as
`bU="28.8" cU="-14.4" cV="-14.4"` over `length="23.35459442"`. Each end was
compared with the end of the road its successor link names:

| Reading | Gap to the linked road | Curve length |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| `normalized` (`p` in [0, 1]) | 0 to 7.3e-8 m | 23.374443 m |
| `arcLength` (`p` in [0, `length`]) | 1.06e4 m | 10,674 m |

So `netconvert` writes normalized coefficients when it omits `pRange`. The
curve length differs from `@length` by 0.020 m (0.085%), within the range
FMT-02 found for tool-written `normalized` files. The unattributed 1.4 files
of FMT-02 were not re-examined.

## 4. Chord bounds for the new shapes

The tessellation bound of
[ADR 0004 §3](../adr/0004-opendrive-geometry-retention.md#3-tessellation)
assumes a constant curvature. Two candidate bounds for the plan-view term
were checked against the largest distance between an offset border and the
chord of one part, sampled at 401 points.

**Spiral.** Random parts with end curvatures from 1e-4 to 0.3 1/m of one
sign (parts are split where the curvature changes sign), lengths from 0.3 to
30 m, heading changes up to π/2, and lateral offsets up to 20 m outside the
turn or up to 0.9 of the smallest radius inside it.

| Bound | Cases | Largest ratio to the bound | Violations |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| ADR 0004's `E_arc` with `K` the larger end curvature: `(1/K + t_max) · (1 − cos(K·h/2))` | 2,458 | 0.998 | 0 |
| Triangle bound (below) | 2,458 | 0.533 | 0 |

**`paramPoly3`.** Random cubics over 5 to 80 m, with `bU` equal to the
length and the other coefficients up to 0.6 of it, parts of 1% to 30% of the
parameter range that contain no inflection (no sign change of `u'v'' −
v'u''`), heading changes below π/2, and offsets up to 6 m on either side.

| Bound | Cases | Largest ratio to the bound | Violations |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Triangle bound: `(c + 2·|t|·sin(Θ/2)) / 2 · tan(Θ/2)` | 3,799 | 0.786 | 0 |

`c` is the chord of the reference line between the part's ends and `Θ` its
heading change. A curve whose tangent turns one way by `Θ < π` lies in the
triangle formed by its chord and its end tangents, whose height is at most
`c/2 · tan(Θ/2)`. A border at a constant offset `t` turns by the same angle,
and its chord is at most `c + 2|t|·sin(Θ/2)`.

## 5. Cross-section bound with superelevation

A rolled border has the horizontal offset `F·cos φ` and the height
`F·sin φ` above the reference line, where `F` is its offset measured in the
rolled plane and `φ` the superelevation; both are cubics in `s`. The
candidate bound for the distance between a rolled border and its chord in
the `(t, z)` plane over a part of length `h` is

```text
h²/8 · ( max|F''| + S·max|F''| + (1 + S)·(2·max|F'|·max|φ'| + max|F|·max|φ''|) + 2·max|F|·max|φ'|² )
```

with `S` the largest `|sin φ|` on the part. The first term is ADR 0004's
term for the same border; the rest vanishes when `φ` is zero on the part.
Random cubics with `|F|` up to 15 m, `|φ|` below 1.4 rad, slopes of `φ` up
to 0.03 rad/m, and parts of 0.3 to 50 m:

| Cases | Largest ratio to the bound | Violations |
| ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 2,737 | 0.969 | 0 |

## 6. Specification text

From the [ASAM OpenDRIVE 1.9.0 specification](https://publications.pages.asam.net/standards/ASAM_OpenDRIVE/ASAM_OpenDRIVE_Specification/v1.9.0/specification/index.html):

- §10.5.2: superelevation is "the roll angle of the road cross section
  around the road reference line", with "positive values for roads falling
  to the right side". It is a cubic in `s`, in radians.
- §8.3: "Values of t are measured in the superelevated lane. The value of
  the @width attribute of the lane does not change if superelevation is
  applied."
- §11.7.4: a lane with `level="true"` "is excluded from superelevation, road
  shape definition and cross section surface definitions of the road", and
  "the elevation of the lane stays on the same height as the outer border of
  the inner connecting lane". All lanes further out shall also be level.
- §9.4: the curvature of a spiral "is linear from the start to the end";
  `curvStart` and `curvEnd` "should not be the same".
- §9.6: `pRange` is mandatory, `normalized` for `p` in [0, 1] and
  `arcLength` for `p` in [0, `@length`]; "there is a non-linear relation
  between the interpolation parameter p and the actual length of the arc";
  the curve length "should match @length". No default is stated, and §5
  (backward compatibility) does not mention `pRange`.
- No text was found on how the lane offset interacts with superelevation.

## 8. Not examined

- The method compiled with a C++ compiler; GEO-02 repeats §2 and §7 there.
- Heading error of the spiral: the heading is a closed-form quadratic, so
  only position was measured.
- The unattributed 1.4 files without `pRange` (§3), and producers other than
  SUMO that omit it.
- Lane-width continuity across lane sections, geometric continuity between
  linked roads, and jumps between profile records, the questions
  [ADR 0004 §6](../adr/0004-opendrive-geometry-retention.md#6-discontinuities)
  left open. ADR 0010 §7 lists them as evidence still needed.
- Tessellation with the bounds of §4 and §5 combined on a whole road; GEO-02
  and XS-01 measure it as GEO-01 did for ADR 0004.
